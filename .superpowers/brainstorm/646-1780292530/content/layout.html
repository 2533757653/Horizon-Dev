Dashboard Layout Direction

How should the three main panels (Kline Chart, Position, Order Place) be arranged?

A

Top-Down Stack

Chart on top (full width), Position and Order Place split below in two columns. Classic trading terminal layout.

B

Left Sidebar

Chart takes left ~65%, Position and Order stacked vertically on the right ~35%. Good for widescreen monitors.

C

Tabbed / Accordion

Chart always visible top, Position and Order Place toggle or collapse below. Maximum chart space.
